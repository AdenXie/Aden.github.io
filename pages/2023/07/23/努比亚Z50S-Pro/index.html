Nubia Z50S Pro: An all-round flagship for flat-screen imaging, creating the art of light and shadow.

The Nubia Z50S Pro is Nubia's latest flagship flat-screen imaging phone, officially released on July 20, 2023. The phone is equipped with the world's first Snapdragon 8 Gen2 leading edition processor, features a 1.5K Super Retina-grade eye-protecting flat screen, a 5100mAh ultra-large battery, and 80W fast charging, and also adopts a new generation of 35mm custom lens module. The major upgrade brings a larger aperture and a brand-new sensor, revolutionizing the imaging experience. In addition, this phone has also promoted the popularization of 1TB of memory in flagship phones, so that users do not need to worry about insufficient storage space. In terms of appearance design, the camera draws on elements of classic professional cameras, adopting a calfskin glass disc camera design, which combines artistic beauty with technological texture. This article will provide you with a detailed introduction to the features and advantages of the Nubia Z50S Pro.

35mm high-cut optics exceed one inch

The biggest highlight of the Nubia Z50S Pro is its new generation of 35mm high-definition optical system, which allows every component to maximize its advantages, exceeding a single inch.123

  • The new phone's 35mm main camera has the largest aperture in the industry.5.21mmThe light intake is increased by **34% compared to the previous generation, and the optical bokeh effect, night scene imaging effect, and snapshot speed have been further upgraded.
  • The new sensor has a 56% improved light sensitivity. At the same shooting distance, using the same pixel lens and the same composition as the 35mm focal length lens, the Nubia Z50S Pro has higher pixel density and clarity, and the image quality surpasses that of a one-inch flagship main camera.
  • image module size22.5*22*6.6mmIt is comparable in size to the module size of the industry's mainstream one-inch large-sensor main camera.
  • In addition, the 35mm main camera was also customized.1G+6P Professional Optical Lens, Abbe number up to81.6It can achieve ultra-low dispersion and ultra-low temperature bleeding, effectively improve purple edges when taking photos, reduce the impact of temperature on the lens, and result in more stable imaging.

In addition, the Nubia Z50S Pro also features a 50MP AF ultra-wide-angle ultra-macro 2-in-1 main camera with a 13mm equivalent focal length, a 125-degree ultra-wide-angle lens, and supports 2.5cm macro shooting; It also features an AF telephoto lens with an 80mm equivalent focal length and supports OIS optical image stabilization.

In terms of imaging module design, the Nubia Z50S Pro pays homage to classic professional cameras, adopting a humanistic eye design. The circular lens module acts as a "focus of light," paired with Nubia's classic double red circle waistline, making the lens full of recognition. Its edges also feature a special three-dimensional carved CD texture, which can present rich changes in light and shadow as the light flows, blending artistic beauty with technological texture.

Computational Photography Upgrade: Humanistic Street Shooting 2.0 - Faster, More Powerful, and More Comprehensive

Customized optics is the foundation of imaging hardware, while computational photography is the sublimation of software. The Nubia Z50S Pro integrates various mirrorless camera shooting techniques and launches the new Humanistic Street Photography 2.0 feature.12

  • With custom slide buttons, users can quickly launch street photography mode and easily capture wonderful moments with the help of the new Lightning Snapshot 4.0.
  • The new phone features a fully upgraded super-sensitive focus tracking function, which can accurately capture faces, bodies, and pets, enabling fast and accurate focusing during the snapshot process.
  • Drawing inspiration from professional cameras, the Nubia Z50S Pro adds a surround photography feature, allowing users to obtain three images with different parameters in just one shot.
  • The new Night Xiao Algorithm 2.0 and Galaxy Super Night 2.0 further optimize night scene shooting and starry sky photography, resulting in more delicate and clear low-light images.
  • Nubia's optical super-resolution algorithm improves the sharpness of telephoto lenses by 30%.
  • The new UI incorporates nubia red elements, the humanistic street photography control area features the leather texture of a professional camera, and the camera family supports a brand-new custom interface, making it look good and take better photos.

The world's first batch of Snapdragon 8 Gen2 leading editions set a new performance benchmark.

As one of the world's first flagship phones equipped with the Snapdragon 8 Gen2 leading edition processor, the Nubia Z50S Pro chip upgrades the clock speed of the Cortex-X3 ultra-large core to 3.36GHz and the GPU clock speed to 719MHz.12

It features a unique Lingxi performance engine that can adaptively adjust the CPU, GPU, and a range of hardware units, achieving optimal performance matching through a deeply refined scheduling mechanism.

To facilitate performance release, the new phone is equipped with a 4233mm2 ultra-large area biomimetic design VC, as well as multiple layers of heat dissipation materials such as diamond thermal gel, nano-carbon fiber, and graphene, with a total heat dissipation area of up to 42,833mm2.

The back of the device is also equipped with nine high-precision NTC temperature sensors, which can sensitively detect subtle temperature changes and adjust the heat dissipation strategy in real time.

In terms of gaming experience, the new device is equipped with the Lingxi Game Hub, which meticulously manages computing power and rendering requirements during the game process, mobilizes computing resources as needed, and features an e-sports-grade game frame stabilization engine that effectively improves game frame rate stability, reduces lag caused by fluctuations and frame drops, and results in a smoother picture.

The esports-grade game space not only provides a variety of support tools and plugin libraries, but also a variety of fun features, making the gaming experience even cooler.

1.5K Super Retina-grade eye-protecting flat screen display with balanced power consumption

In terms of screen, the Nubia Z50S Pro features a 6.78-inch AMOLED eye-protecting flat screen with a top-tier blue diamond arrangement, which can achieve an ultra-high resolution of 2800×1260 and a super-retina-level high pixel density of 452PPI; It features a cinema-level brilliant color display with 100% coverage of the DCI-P3 color gamut, 10-bit color depth, 1.07 billion color ranges, and a contrast ratio of 8000000:1; Supports 120Hz refresh rate and 1000Hz instantaneous touch sampling rate.

  • While boasting excellent display performance, the Nubia Z50S Pro screen also achieves balanced power consumption, featuring Q9 luminescent material, which reduces power consumption by 15% compared to the previous generation of luminescent materials.
  • The tempered glass on the screen uses a deep multi-reinforcement process to provide hard-core drop resistance.
  • To protect eye health, the new screen also supports 2160Hz high-frequency PWM dimming and global DC dimming, making the visual experience easier and effectively reducing visual fatigue.

The classic design of a professional camera, the Mirror of Light Special Edition, illuminates the "art of light".

In terms of appearance, the Nubia Z50S Pro features two classic color options: black and khaki. It draws inspiration from the classic rangefinder camera's metal casing paired with a leather outer shell, creating a dual-texture back cover that can simultaneously present AG glitter sand and leather texture effects.

  • The glass surface uses a unique three-dimensional etching process to recreate the delicate feel of calfskin, making it cooler and more comfortable to the touch. It is also wear-resistant, slip-resistant, stain-resistant, and fingerprint-resistant.
  • The device features a metal frame and a natural R-angle design, is only 8.6mm thick, and weighs approximately 228 grams.

Based on the concept that "photography is the art of light," Nubia has also created a special edition of the Z50S Pro Light Mirror.

  • The back case features an Italian "Carrara White" natural marble texture design, and uses 3D laser photolithography and nano-level coating to restore the natural crystal texture of the raw stone.
  • The rear camera module features a rainbow holographic texture transparent ring design, which can bloom with unique and vibrant colors under different lighting angles, expressing Nubia's ultimate pursuit of light and shadow art.

The light and clean MyOS13 system helps users use it smoothly.

The Nubia Z50S Pro is equipped with the MyOS13 system, which is light, clean, fun, and easy to use.

  • Lightweight firmware, most pre-installed applications can be uninstalled, few ads, and the system ad switch is turned off by default.
  • The system has also upgraded the highly praised charging separation function in the game space to global system support, supporting only power supply without charging in more scenarios, maintaining a low-temperature feel while charging and playing, and bringing good body temperature and performance supply.

Price and purchase method

The Nubia Z50S Pro will officially go on sale on July 27 on ZTE Mall, JD.com, Tmall, Douyin, JD.com Home, JD.com Computers & Digital, JD.com Appliances Super Experience Store, and other platforms.

  • The new 12GB+256GB version is priced at 3699 yuan;
  • Four 1TB memory versions have been launched: the standard 12GB+1TB version is priced at 3999 yuan, and the 16GB+1TB version is priced at 4399 yuan; The Mirror of Light Special Edition 12GB+1TB is priced at 4099 yuan, and the 16GB+1TB version is priced at 4499 yuan.
