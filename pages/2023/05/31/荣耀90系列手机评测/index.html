Honor 90 series mobile phone review

AdenXie
  2023-05-31 21:49:26 2023-05-31 21:49:26 establish   2023-05-31 21:55:50 2023-05-31 21:55:50 update

Honor 90 series phone review: 200-megapixel main camera, 3840Hz high-frequency PWM dimming screen, combining thinness and battery life.

The Honor 90 series is Honor's latest flagship phone, officially released on May 29, 2023. The Honor 90 series phones come in two models: the Honor 90 and the Honor 90 Pro. Their main features are a 200-megapixel photo camera, a zero-risk dimming eye-protecting screen, a 5000mAh lightweight and long battery life, and the powerful performance of the Snapdragon 7 and Snapdragon 8+ Gen1 chips. So, what are the differences between these two phones? How do they stand out in the competition at the same price point? This article will provide a detailed introduction and comparison of the various configurations of the Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro phones, covering aspects such as design, display, core performance, camera, battery life, and charging, to help you make a more informed purchasing decision.

Appearance design

The Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro showcase distinctive features in their exterior design. The front of the Honor 90 features a curved screen with a centered hole. This hole is relatively small and located in the center of the top of the screen, giving it a simple and smooth feel. The "figure-eight" camera module design on the back adds a unique touch to the overall look. The Honor 90 also supports NFC, making it convenient for users to perform near-field communication. The device is relatively thin and light, weighing 183g and 7.8mm thick, making it more comfortable to hold and easier to operate.

In contrast, the front of the Honor 90 Pro features a pill-shaped punch-out curved screen. This punch-out design is relatively large and located in the center of the top of the screen, giving it a unique sense of technology. The "figure-eight" camera module design on the back is basically the same as that of the Honor 90, showcasing the brand's consistent style. Like the Honor 90, the Honor 90 Pro also supports NFC, making it convenient for users' daily use. However, the Honor 90 Pro is relatively heavy, weighing 192g and 8.1mm thick, which may feel slightly heavy when held in the hand.

display screen

Both the Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro use OLED technology in terms of displays, providing an excellent visual experience. The Honor 90 features a 6.7-inch screen with a resolution of 2664x1200 pixels, ensuring a detailed display. Meanwhile, the Honor 90's screen also boasts an impressive 120Hz refresh rate, adding a sense of realism to the smooth sliding and animation effects. The peak brightness of up to 1600 nits makes the Honor 90 clearly visible even in bright environments. In addition, it uses 3840Hz high-frequency PWM dimming technology to provide a more comfortable visual experience.

The Honor 90 Pro is equipped with a larger 6.78-inch screen with a resolution of 2700x1224 pixels, presenting more detailed picture details. Like the Honor 90, the Honor 90 Pro also features a 120Hz refresh rate and a peak brightness of 1600 nits, bringing users a smooth and clear visual experience. Similarly, it also uses 3840Hz high-frequency PWM dimming technology to provide more comfortable screen brightness adjustment.

core performance

The Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro have significant differences in core performance. The Honor 90 is equipped with the Snapdragon 7 Gen1 chip, which is a mid-range chip based on the 6nm process. It has 4 A78 large cores and 4 A55 small cores, with a maximum clock speed of 2.4GHz and an Adreno 642L GPU. While this chip's performance is not weak, it is not outstanding either, and it does not have a significant advantage in the competition at the same price point. The Honor 90 has 8GB or 12GB of RAM and 128GB or 256GB of storage, and supports the UFS 3.1 standard.

The Honor 90 Pro is equipped with the Snapdragon 8+ Gen1 chip, which is a high-end chip based on the 5nm process. It has one A78 ultra-large core, three A78 large cores and four A55 small cores, with a maximum clock speed of 2.84GHz and an Adreno 660 GPU. This chip has very powerful performance and a clear advantage in the competition at the same price point. The Honor 90 Pro has 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage, and also supports the UFS 3.1 standard.

These parameters show that the Honor 90 Pro is much more powerful than the Honor 90 in terms of core performance, with obvious advantages in terms of processor, RAM, and storage. If you are a user with high requirements for phone performance, then the Honor 90 Pro is undoubtedly a better choice.

Camera

The Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro also have different camera configurations. The Honor 90 features a quad-camera rear design: a 200-megapixel main camera, an 8-megapixel ultra-wide-angle camera, a 2-megapixel macro camera, and a 2-megapixel depth-of-field camera. The 200-megapixel main camera uses a Sony IMX800 sensor, supports 9-in-1 pixel merging technology, and can output high-quality photos of 22.56 megapixels. The 8-megapixel ultra-wide-angle camera supports a 123° ultra-wide-angle field of view, allowing you to capture a wider range of landscapes. The 2-megapixel macro camera supports 4cm close-up focus and can photograph subtle objects. A 2-megapixel depth-of-field camera can provide better background blur.

The Honor 90 Pro features a five-camera rear setup: a 200-megapixel main camera, a 32-megapixel telephoto camera, an 8-megapixel ultra-wide-angle camera, a 2-megapixel macro camera, and a 2-megapixel depth-of-field camera. The 200-megapixel main camera is the same as the Honor 90, both using a Sony IMX800 sensor. The 32-megapixel telephoto camera is a major highlight of the Honor 90 Pro. It supports 2.5x optical zoom, 10x hybrid zoom, and 100x digital zoom, and has optical image stabilization, allowing you to photograph distant objects. The 8-megapixel ultra-wide-angle camera is the same as the Honor 90, and both support a 123° ultra-wide-angle field of view. The 2-megapixel macro camera and 2-megapixel depth-of-field camera are also the same as those in the Honor 90.

These parameters show that the Honor 90 Pro is more powerful than the Honor 90 in terms of camera, with better performance in both the main camera, telephoto lens, and ultra-wide-angle lens. If you are a user with high requirements for mobile phone photography, then the Honor 90 Pro is undoubtedly a better choice.

Range and charging

The Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro also have different performances in terms of battery life and charging. The Honor 90 is equipped with a large-capacity 5000mAh battery, which can maintain a battery life of more than a day under light use and about half a day² under heavy use. In addition, the Honor 90 also supports 66W wired fast charging technology, which can fully charge the battery in 35 minutes.

The Honor 90 Pro is also equipped with a large-capacity 5000mAh battery, which is basically equivalent to the Honor 90 in terms of battery life. However, the Honor 90 Pro is faster than the Honor 90 in terms of charging speed. It supports 90W wired fast charging technology and can fully charge the battery in 28 minutes.

These parameters show that there is not much difference between the Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro in terms of battery life. Both have a large-capacity 5000mAh battery and have good power consumption control capabilities. However, in terms of charging speed, the Honor 90 Pro is faster than the Honor 90 and comes with a 100W charger².

sum up

By comparing the above aspects, we can see that the Honor 90 and Honor 90 Pro each have their own advantages and disadvantages. If you have high requirements for your phone's performance, camera, and charging speed, and don't mind a slightly heavier body and a larger, more prominent punch-hole screen design, then you can choose to buy the Honor 90 Pro. If you have high requirements for your phone's performance, camera, and charging speed, and don't mind a slightly heavier body and a larger, more prominent punch-hole screen design, then you can choose to buy the Honor 90 Pro. If you don't have high requirements for your phone's performance, camera, or charging speed, but prefer a thinner and lighter body and a smaller, more discreet punch-hole screen design, then you can choose to buy the Honor 90. Of course, regardless of which model you choose, you can enjoy the biggest highlight of the Honor 90 series – the 3840Hz high-frequency PWM dimming screen. This is currently the only phone on the market with this technology, which can provide you with a more comfortable visual experience.

In conclusion, the Honor 90 series is a mobile phone with an innovative spirit and strong performance, which performs well in terms of appearance design, display, core performance, camera, battery life and charging. If you are looking for a phone that can meet your daily needs while bringing you surprises and fun, then the Honor 90 series is worth considering and buying.
