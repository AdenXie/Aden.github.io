Xiaomi 14 Ultra Overview

The Xiaomi 14 Ultra is a flagship imaging phone with excellent photography performance, powerful hardware, and a unique design. Let's understand it from multiple perspectives.

1. Photographic performance

  • four-camera systemThe Xiaomi 14 Ultra is equipped with a full-focal length Leica optical large aperture quad camera, including a main camera, ultra-wide-angle camera, telephoto camera, and macro camera. The combination of these cameras enables users to take excellent photos and videos.
  • Main Camera:Sony LYT 900 1-inch 1.6-micron 50-megapixel (default four-pixel combination) 23 mm focal length f/1.63-f/4.0 Octa-PD phase focus optical image stabilization 8P lens
  • Ultra-wide angle and macro:Sony IMX 858 1/2.51 inch 50 megapixels 122°5 cm macro 12 mm focal length f/1.8 autofocus
  • Telephoto:Sony IMX 858 1/2.51-inch 50-megapixel 75 mm focal length macro f/1.8 3x optical zoom optical image stabilization 10 cm or more autofocus
  • Telephoto:Sony IMX858 1/2.51-inch 50-megapixel 120 mm periscope telephoto macro f/2.55x optical zoom 30 cm or more autofocus optical image stabilization
  • Xiaomi AISPThis phone is equipped with the world's first AI large-scale model computational photography architecture, Xiaomi AISP, which optimizes image processing through AI algorithms to improve image quality and detail. In actual testing, the "Super Telephoto Beta" triggered at over 30x can greatly optimize image clarity.

2. Design and Materials

  • Xiaomi Dragon Armor ArchitectureThe Xiaomi 14 Ultra adopts the brand-new "Xiaomi Dragon Armor Architecture," featuring a high-strength aluminum alloy frame and Xiaomi Dragon Crystal Glass. The bending resistance of the device frame is 8% improved compared to the iPhone's titanium frame, and the drop resistance of the glass is 10 times improved.
  • appearanceThe phone has an exquisite appearance and is available in Dragon Crystal Ceramic Blue, Vegan White, and Vegan Black to meet the aesthetic needs of different users.

3. Other characteristics

  • satellite communicationThe Xiaomi 14 Ultra is Xiaomi's first phone to support satellite communication, using the Tiantong satellite communication solution to bring users a greater sense of security.
  • screenThe C8 high-quality 1-120Hz LTPO OLED from CSOT has a screen resolution of 3200 x 1440 pixels, presenting clear and sharp images.
  • performanceEquipped with a Qualcomm third-generation Snapdragon 8 processor, Hynix memory, and Samsung fast storage, it is suitable for multitasking and gaming.
  • Battery:The 5300mAh Xiaomi GSR battery and MIFC 2.0 protocol fast flash charging with a maximum wired capacity of 90W and a maximum wireless capacity of 80W provide long battery life and ultra-fast charging.
  • Fingerprint recognition:Ultra-thin under-display fingerprint sensor provides faster fingerprint recognition.
  • Speakers and motors:The SLA1010 massive X-axis linear motor and 1014+1115D speakers from AAC Technologies deliver a powerful listening and tactile feel.
  • System:The new XiaomiHyperOS, based on the Android 14 kernel
