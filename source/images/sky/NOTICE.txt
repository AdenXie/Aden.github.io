milkyway-2048-v1.webp / milkyway-4096-v1.webp:
NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020.
Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. Hipparcos and Tycho-2: ESA.
Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
Original: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004800/a004851/milkyway_2020_4k.exr
Derived by subtracting a black point, gamma encoding, a light blur, resampling and
encoding WebP.
Usage: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The fainter "dust" stars in stars-v1.bin are generated, not catalogued: positions are
drawn at random where the Milky Way image above is bright.

Planet positions are computed in the browser from JPL's "Approximate Positions of the
Planets" (E. M. Standish) and magnitudes from Mallama & Hilton (2018).
